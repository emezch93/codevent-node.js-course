Privacy

This course application has no account system beyond the access token used to unlock it, and no server of its own. It does not collect, transmit, or store your data on any server it controls.

What Is Stored

Your course access token, lesson progress, completed items, checklist state, and last-viewed lesson are saved using your browser's local storage. This data stays on your device and browser only. Clearing your browser data or local storage will remove your access token and reset your progress, requiring you to re-enter your token.

What Is Not Collected

The app does not use analytics, cookies, or tracking scripts. It does not ask for your name or email directly within the course pages.

Access Token Verification

When your access token is checked, it is sent to a Cloudflare Worker operated by CodeVent Digital for verification. The Worker verifies the token's signature and validity and does not store it.
